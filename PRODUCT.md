# Mlo: An Intelligent School Feeding Management and Communication System

Product brief.
## Register

product

## Users

School staff record deliveries and serve meals during a busy service window. The user confirmed phone-first meal service on 2026-11-09; desktop reporting is secondary for the school pilot. Suppliers plan and deliver food, county staff review programme exceptions and payment records, and administrators resolve operational issues.

## Product Purpose

Mlo connects forecast demand, dispatch, school receipt, meal records, guardian communication and reconciliation. Success means staff can identify their school and date, see the task needing attention, complete it and understand the result. Meal records describe collection, not proof that food was eaten. Payment-file exports prepare payment information without transferring money.

## Brand Personality

Warm, grounded, plain-spoken. Communicate authority without sounding clinical. Use short declaratives, clear quantity labels and verb-led actions. This is an operations tool used under time pressure.

## Anti-references

Clinical dashboards, decorative marketing inside service tasks, identical card grids that obscure priority, unexplained technical errors, and charts without a decision they support. These pattern exclusions follow AGENTS.md and the revamp roadmap; no named competitor preference has been supplied.

## Design Principles

1. Establish school, workspace and date before asking staff to act.
2. Put urgent exceptions and the next service action before supporting reports.
3. Keep one authoritative figure for each quantity; preserve forecast, dispatched, received, confirmed and served distinctions.
4. Explain historical viewing versus current-day recording at the action.
5. Preserve existing permissions, reader persistence and offline recovery while simplifying presentation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve 44px minimum controls, visible keyboard focus, labelled inputs, announced feedback, reduced motion and 200% text support. Never communicate status with colour alone. Phone landscape, long names and intermittent connectivity are required review cases. Real NFC and assistive-technology checks remain separate from browser fixtures.
